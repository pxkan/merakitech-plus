# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and constructive, assume good intent, no harassment or personal attacks, and keep
discussions about the project. Everyone is welcome regardless of background or experience — this is
a hobby project made by and for coffee enthusiasts.

Problems can be reported privately to the maintainer (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for contact
details). The maintainer may edit or remove content and block participants who do not follow this code.
