# Meraki+ v7.1: design-polish spec

v7.1 refines the v7.0 OEM look: copper with a fine guilloche, thin white rings, Title Case names curved along the
rings, white line icons, and white on copper. Nothing new is introduced. The spec synthesizes four parallel
critiques of the real renders (HOME and its states, feature pages and the editor, the live view and DONE, and
SYSTEM / DIAGNOSTICS / misc) and is the contract for the implementation. Display and navigation only: nothing
that reaches the UART changes.

Every coordinate is in screen px on the 480×480 framebuffer. The centre is (240, 240) and the bezel is at r = 234.
"Cap top" is the y that `text_at` takes; "cap" is the style's cap height.

## 1. Safe area: three rings

| Zone | Radius | What may live there |
|---|---|---|
| **Content** | r ≤ 206 | All text, icons, pills, buttons, rows, the chart. A centred text line is fitted to the chord `2·√(206² − dy²)` at its farthest row (`chord_w(y0, y1)`). |
| **Rings** | r ≤ 206 | The HOME rings and their arc labels (the outer edge of the label glyphs counts). |
| **Edge furniture** | 212 ≤ r ≤ 222 | Only thin non-text marks: the top chevron, the live view's progress ring, the scroll indicator, and the splash ring. Never text. |

Black outside r = 234 (bezel), as before.

## 2. Type scale: 8 roles, 3 weights (Bold is retired)

| Role | Face | Size (em) | Cap | Tabular | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Caption` | Medium | 15 | 10.5 | yes | sub-lines, range / status / note lines, toasts, stat labels, DIAGNOSTICS labels, Start caption |
| `Body` | Medium | 17 | 11.9 | yes | live phase line, live sub line, ring targets, status text that must be read |
| `Label` | SemiBold | 18 | 12.6 | no (clock: yes) | ring names on the arc (tracking 0.6), the HOME clock |
| `Row` | Medium | 20 | 14.0 | yes | list row labels, enum neighbours on the roller |
| `Value` | SemiBold | 20 | 14.0 | yes | list row values, buttons (STOP, OK, pills), DIAGNOSTICS section captions |
| `Title` | SemiBold | 26 | 18.2 | yes | page / editor / action titles, the error meaning, the selected enum word on the roller |
| `Numeral` | Medium | 26 | 18.2 | yes | the live stats, the live hero's unit; Light 26 for roller neighbours and the roller unit |
| `Hero` | SemiBold | 36 | 25.2 | no | the one word in the system actions ("Original" / "Meraki+"), the splash wordmark |
| `Roller` | Light | 60 | 42 | yes | the roller's selected number (and a sensor value in a read-only editor) |
| `Display` | Medium | 88 | 61.6 | proportional digits, anchored at the decimal point | the live view's big number |

Rules:
- **Hierarchy comes from size first, then colour (white, then cream), then weight.** A row is a Medium label next to a
  SemiBold value, and never Bold.
- **A unit uses its number's face, about ⅓ of its size, in cream, on the same baseline** (row: 20 → Caption 15; roller:
  Light 60 → Light 26; live hero: Medium 88 → Numeral 26). The 3 px gap before a unit is `0.1·cap` of the unit style.
- **Retired:** `F_TINY` (13), `F_MB` (Bold), `F_CLK` (Bold), `F_NUM`, `F_XL`, `F_STAT` (Light 30), and `F_XXL` (Light 112).
  The old names may stay as aliases while code is migrated, but they must map to a role above.

## 3. Spacing scale

4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48. Rows use a 52 px pitch and the roller a 64 px pitch. A gap between two text lines is
measured cap bottom to cap top. Rule: related lines 8–12, separate blocks 16–24, a heading above its content 24
over / 12 under.

## 4. Colour (unchanged tokens; usage tightened)

- White `C_TXT` for anything you act on or read as data; cream `C_TXT2` for secondary text and units; `C_TXT3` for captions.
- **Yellow means blocked, failed or out of spec, and only that** (`C_WARN` / `C_ERR`), always with the ⚠ icon. Plain
  information (estimate hints, "No effect …") is cream.
- The dark espresso layer with the red ring stays for errors and power.

## 5. Background

- Vignette: the edge term drops from −50/−30/−16 to **−22/−13/−7**, with no extra dark band at the rim. The copper stays
  copper all the way out.
- Guilloche: **flat amplitude (~4 %)** from the centre to the rim, a **5.0 px pitch**, a soft cosine line profile, and the
  distance a blend of a **sharp square (Chebyshev)** and the circle, so the lines crease on the diagonals like stock's
  seams instead of forming round contours that beat into moiré.

## 6. Components

### 6.1 Header (every page, editor, action)
- **Chevron:** 14 × 6 px, half-width 1.0, α 160 (pressed: α 255 plus a disc r 18 α 28), apex at y 26. Its tap area is unchanged.
- **Title:** `Title` style, cap top **y 44**, fitted to `chord_w` (≈ 196 px wide). If it does not fit, fall back to
  `Value` 20; if that does not fit either, shorten the copy (no "…" in titles).
- **Sub-line:** `Caption`, cap top **y 76**, fitted to the chord. **The toast slot of a page is the sub-line** (§6.9).

### 6.2 HOME rings
- `RING_R` **66**, centres **(240 ± 84, 240 ± 84)**. The gap between rings is 36 px on both axes, the outer ring edge sits at r 185 and the label glyphs end near r 196.
- Stroke half-width 1.4. The heating arc uses the same half-width as the ready ring (the ring must never get *thinner* when it turns ready), with the track at α 70.
- **Label:** `Label` 18 SemiBold, tracking 0.6, at 225° / 315° / 135° / 45°, upper tops outward and lower tops inward (stock's
  rule, kept). Gap = half the label's arc plus 9 px (converted to degrees at the ring radius).
- **Icon:** drawn on a 36 px box, **centred on its ink box** (not on its advance), ink centre at cy − 12, stroke 2.4 (matches
  the ring visually). The four icons have the same optical weight:
  - bean pair at 0.9×
  - drop at 1.08×
  - **cup redrawn upright** with a handle and a saucer no wider than the cup + 8
  - **steam = three rising wisps over a short base line** (the cloud with an arrow read as "download")
- **Target** inside: `Body` 17, white α 235, cap top cy + 16.
- **Hold to start:** pressed disc α 28 plus an **inner arc at r − 7, half-width 1.3**, from 12 o'clock. The same rule
  applies to every ring button (Start, OK).
- **Dimmed** (offline, sleep): rings α 110, labels α 120, targets `C_DIS`.

### 6.3 HOME furniture
- **Status line** (heating, blocked, offline, pre-heat, a result): `Caption`, cap centre **y 62**, fitted to the chord (≈ 230 px). Short
  copy: "No connection", "Heating · 64° → 87°" …
- **Clock:** `Label` 18 SemiBold tabular, cap centre **y 392**, between the lower rings (stock). 12 h: "4:35 PM".
- **Toasts on HOME use the status slot** and never cover a ring.
- **Error layer:** the red ring (r 40) at y 168, then the meaning (`Title`, e.g. "Brew boiler sensor fault", 2 lines max), then
  `Caption` "E2 · Tap for details". The code appears once.

### 6.4 Live view (an activity runs, or its DONE)
Cap tops: phase line **64** (`Body`, cream) · hero **92** (`Display` 88, cap 62) · sub line **172** (`Body`, cream) =
toast slot · chart **204–268** · stat values **290** (`Numeral` 26) · stat labels **316** (`Caption`, C_TXT3) ·
bottom slot **pill 356–412**.

- **Hero:** Medium 88, proportional digits laid out on the decimal point (the point stays put while the digits change). Unit: `Numeral` 26, cream, baseline-aligned, gap 6.
- **Edge progress ring:** r 220, half-width 1.1, track white α 40, from −90° + 9° clockwise over 342° (a gap at 12 o'clock
  for the chevron). Hidden for the wand (no target, so there is no progress to show).
- **Chart** (248 × 64 at x 116–364):
  - The time axis is **shot time**: from the activity start, span = max(30 s, elapsed × 1.1). DONE shows the whole shot edge to edge.
  - The main curve is **weight** (brew, water, grind) or **milk temperature** (steam): white, 2 px, smooth (sample to sample with
    capsules, a 3-tap average), with a soft white fill α 0 → 26.
  - The main curve is scaled so the **target sits at 85 % of the height**, drawn as a dashed cream line (dash 4, gap 4, α 160).
  - **Pressure** (brew only) is a faint secondary curve: 1.2 px cream α 120, a fixed 0–12 bar scale, and no fill.
  - Baseline: 1 px white α 60 across the full width. **No text labels** in the chart (no "Pressure / Weight / - - Target").
  - Raw mode: the curve is drawn raw and a small "Raw" caption in yellow sits at the top right of the chart (power-user view, kept).
- **Stats:** 3 columns at a 100 px pitch.
  - Value: `Numeral` Medium 26, white, with its unit as a `Caption` suffix in cream ("19.2 s", "15.1 bar", "99.9°").
  - Label: `Caption` C_TXT3, a plain noun ("Time", "Pressure", "Flow", "Boiler", "Milk", "Level", "Current", "Step").
  - "≈bar" is gone: pressure reads "≈15.1" with the unit "bar".
- **Bottom slot** (always says how this ends):
  - **STOP**: a solid white pill, one at 200 × 56; two at 136 × 48 (8 px gap), at y 360.
  - **Wand**: the instruction "Release the wand to stop" (`Body`, cream) at cap centre y 384.
  - **DONE**: an outline "Close" pill in the STOP rect (the DONE tap target, now visible).
- **DONE:**
  - Phase "Done".
  - Hero = the result.
  - Sub line = the target ("Target 36.0 g", steam auto: "Target 65.0° · rises ≈4° more").
  - Stats: **two**, at ±60 px: Time and the peak pressure (brew), Time and Boiler (water / steam), Time and Target (grind). No "0 Flow".

### 6.5 List rows (feature pages, SYSTEM)
- **Field:** x 80–400; y **100–322** on feature pages (above Start) and **100–396** on SYSTEM. Edge fade 16 px.
- **Pitch:** 52. Label `Row` at x 92, white (cream when locked). Value `Value` right-aligned at **x 388**. Unit `Caption` cream, baseline-aligned, gap 3.
- **No chevron on setting rows.** A chevron (›, cream) appears only on rows that lead elsewhere: SYSTEM section headers
  (› closed, ⌄ open), the DIAGNOSTICS entry, and the two system actions.
- **No separators on feature pages.** On SYSTEM, one hairline (α 28, x 96–384) above each section header. **No side bar** on an open section; the 14 px indent is enough.
- **Pressed:** a rounded rect from x 80 to 400, height = pitch − 8, radius 14, white α 30.
- **Short lists are centred** in their field.
- Out of spec: the value in `C_ERR` with ⚠ (kept).
- No "≈" before values in rows. The editor's note line carries "Upper limit is approximate".
- **Scroll indicator:** r 216, ±16° around 3 o'clock; track half-width 0.8 α 28, thumb half-width 1.1 α 140.

### 6.6 Start (feature pages)
- Ring r 34 at (240, 362). Caption "Hold to Start" in `Caption` cream, cap top 408. Play icon centred on its ink.
- Hold progress: the inner arc at r − 7 (§6.2 rule).

### 6.7 Editor (roller)
- Header as in §6.1. The range line is the sub-line (y 76), en dash: "85.0–96.0 °C".
- **3 visible values.** Pitch 64, centre line **y 196**, window ±100 with a 24 px fade, so the ±2 values show only while
  dragging.
- Selected number: `Roller` Light 60, white. Unit: Light 26, cream, on the baseline.
- Neighbours: Light 26, cream, α 150.
- Selected word (enum): `Title` 26 SemiBold. Word neighbours: `Row` Medium 20, cream α 150. A neighbour is never larger than the selection.
- **−/+**: ring r 28 at x **74 / 406**, y 196. That gives the text 244 px of room.
- **Status line** (`Caption`): cap top 290. **Note line** (`Caption`): cap top 310.
- **OK**: ring r 34 at y 376, filled white once the value has changed. A read-only editor says "Close".

### 6.8 System actions (Boot UI / Switch / exit reminder)
Stack (cap tops): title 44 · sub 76 · hero word centre 184 (−/+ rings on the same line for Boot UI) · status 254 ·
note 276 · pills **312–360** (136 × 48 each, 16 px gap, centred on CX) · one footer line 382 (`Caption`, fitted).
One hero style (`Hero` 36 SemiBold) on every action screen. Developer references ("see BOOT.md §7") are removed from the device.

### 6.9 Toast
- An espresso pill, height **30**, radius 15, `Caption` text, ⚠ in yellow for warnings. Width ≤ the chord at its top row minus 32.
- **Slot = the screen's sub-line:** HOME = the status line (centre y 62); pages and editors = the sub-line (centre y 81);
  live view = the sub line (centre y 178). The sub-line underneath is not drawn while the toast shows. A toast
  never covers a title, a row, a ring, the chart or the stats.

### 6.10 DIAGNOSTICS
- One calm column.
  - **Inline row:** label `Caption` cream at x 104 and value `Body` white right-aligned at x 376, pitch 28.
  - **Stacked row** (paths, logs, long values): the label, then the value 18 px below, both left at x 104, fitted to 272.
- Section captions: `Value` 20, white, left-aligned at x 104, 24 px above and 8 px below, with no hairlines.
- Plain labels, sentence case; the technical value stays (`Startup sync`, `Machine awake`, `Bad frames · resyncs` …).
- The live chart toggle is the only tap target, as before.

### 6.11 Splash, power, transitions
- The splash ring at r 216, half-width 1.2. The status line shows "v7.1"; the config state appears only when it is missing (yellow, ⚠).
- Version shown as "v7.1" (lower-case v) everywhere on screen.
- Page slide: clip to the fixed screen circle, not each page's own circle (no lens-shaped gaps).

## 7. Copy rules

- **Title Case only for names:** ring labels, page / editor titles, row labels, buttons. Everything that is a phrase or a
  sentence (status, sub-lines, toasts, notes, hints) is sentence case.
- **Each "·"-separated fragment starts with a capital:** "Boiler 99.9° · Group 70°", "Kedel 99.9° · Gruppe 70°".
- No developer references on the device (file names, "word 51", "§7", "/proc/…" as a *label*; values may stay technical on DIAGNOSTICS).
- A range uses an en dash ("85.0–96.0 °C"), a minus is U+2212 ("Hold − or + to go faster").
- "and", not "+", in prose.
- Danish: no English words where Danish has one ("damprør", not "wand").
- One name per concept: the manufacturer's UI is **"Original UI"** everywhere a kitchen user sees it.
- A phase name never repeats the ring label in a different form inside one view.

## 8. Motion (unchanged budget, restrained)
- Transitions stay 150–250 ms ease-out. The hold sweep is linear over HOLD_MS. No new motion is added. The progress ring eases as before.

## 9. Budget
- A brew frame must be no slower than v7.0 on ARM (0.78 ms under qemu). Static layers (background, ring labels, icons,
  titles) stay pre-rendered. The chart is redrawn only inside its widget.
